Import Guide

Complete guide to importing from Japan

🚢 Shipping from Japan

Container Options

  • 20ft Container: ¥150,000 - Fits 1-2 small cars
  • 40ft Container: ¥160,000 - Fits 2-3 medium cars
  • 40ft High Cube: ¥180,000 - Fits 3-4 vehicles

Insurance

Mandatory 1.5% of purchase price protects your investment during transit.

Transit Time

  • Japan to Kingston: 4-6 weeks
  • Customs clearance: 1-3 days after payment

📜 Age Restrictions

  • Motor Cars: Maximum 5 years
  • Pick-up Trucks: Maximum 6 years
  • Hybrid Cars: Maximum 5 years
  • Electric Vehicles: Maximum 3 years
⚠️ Important: Vehicles exceeding age limits will be rejected by Jamaica Customs.

📋 Required Documents

  • Bill of Lading (original)
  • Commercial Invoice
  • Import Declaration Form (C82)
  • Export Certificate from Japan
  • Vehicle Registration Document
  • Tax Compliance Certificate (TCC)
  • Valid ID (Passport or National ID)

Is this calculator free?

Yes! 100% free with no registration required.

Are the rates accurate?

Yes, based on current Jamaica Customs regulations. Always verify with Jamaica Customs for final rates.

What currencies are supported?

🚗 Import Questions

What are the age limits?

  • Motor Cars: 5 years maximum
  • Pick-ups: 6 years maximum
  • Hybrids: 5 years maximum
  • EVs: 3 years maximum

How long does shipping take?

4-6 weeks from Japan to Kingston, plus 1-3 days for customs clearance.

Why are EVs cheaper?

EVs have only 10% total duty (10% import, 0% SCT, 0% GCT) vs much higher rates for gasoline vehicles.
